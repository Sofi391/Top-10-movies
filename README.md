This is a project which uses flask for the backend with bootstrap and SQLALCHAMY.
It allows you to add your favourite movie by searching it by its name
From the provided movies you can choose the one you seek by its release date
You will be prompted to add your rating and review for the movie
Once your have put your reviews it will add it to the page where you can access your top 10 favourite movies ordered by there rating
You can do the same for your favourite show
